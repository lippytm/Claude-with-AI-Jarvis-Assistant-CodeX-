# Licensing — Draft Structure

**Status: DRAFT — not yet reviewed by an attorney. Do not treat as final legal terms.**

This repository's material is split into three categories, each with its own license file, instead of one blanket license:

| Category | File | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Code | `LICENSE-CODE.md` | Source code, scripts, configuration |
| Creative content | `LICENSE-CONTENT.md` | Personas, stories, written/creative material (e.g. Hermes Fabric MemPal content) |
| Commercial terms | `LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md` | Paid products, the AI Jarvis Assistant name/brand, anything sold through the Affiliate Marketing Network |

## Why split it up
Code, creative writing, and sellable products have different risks and different norms — a license that makes sense for open-source code (e.g. permissive reuse) is usually the wrong choice for a commercial product or original creative fiction. Splitting them lets each piece get the protection it actually needs.
